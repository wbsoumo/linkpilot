Cookie Policy

Last Updated: June 25, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how LinkPilot AI deploys cookies and similar session storage mechanisms to recognize you when you visit our landing pages or access the user dashboard.

1. What are Cookies?

Cookies are tiny text records placed on your local drive by web servers. They help platforms remember authorization sessions, active navigation pages, and configuration preferences to make user navigation seamless.

2. Cookies We Deploy

Authentication & Security Tokens (Essential)

We use session cookies and local storage tokens to recognize authenticated users. Without these, you would be forced to log in on every single page redirect.

Performance & Analytics (Non-essential)

We track anonymized usage metrics like total landing page visits and button clicks to continuously optimize platform loading speeds.

3. Managing Cookie Parameters

You can opt to block or clear cookies using your browser's settings panels. Please note that blocking essential cookies will make the dashboard completely inaccessible.
