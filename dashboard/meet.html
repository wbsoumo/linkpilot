Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to LinkPilot AI CRM. We are committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, process, share, and protect your personal information when you use our scheduling booking system, visitor pages, and associated platform modules.

2. Information We Collect

A. Visitor and Guest Data:

When you schedule a meeting with a host through LinkPilot, we collect the details you submit, including your full name, email address, timezone preference, remarks, and any additional guest emails you provide.

B. Host Configuration Data:

For hosts setting up links, we gather sync tokens, timezone choices, weekly availability hours, credentials for external calendar integrations (e.g. Google Calendar, Zoom), and preferences.

C. Device & Technical Metadata:

We automatically log IP addresses, browser dimensions, browser languages, and interaction histories to enhance the security and integrity of our systems.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your information under legal bases including performance of contract, legitimate interest, and explicit consent:

  • To calculate available calendar slots and process meeting scheduling requests.
  • To sync events with Google Calendar and Zoom API endpoints.
  • To send booking confirmation emails containing attachments (e.g. ICS files).
  • To monitor platform stability, troubleshoot bugs, and deter fraudulent activities.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Integrations

We never sell your personal information. Your data is shared only with authorized processors or integrations that you explicitly connect:

  • Google Calendar API / Zoom API: To write scheduled slots onto your connected accounts.
  • SMTP / Mail Services: To deliver notifications and invitations.
  • Legal Compliance: If required by a court subpoena or legal authority.

5. Data Retention & Security

We store your data securely in standard databases using advanced encryption standards. Meeting participant records are retained for as long as necessary to fulfill the host's customer relationship management requirements. You may request data deletion at any time by contacting the host or our support team.

6. GDPR & Privacy Rights

Under European GDPR and California CCPA guidelines, you have the right to access, rectify, restrict, port, or request erasure of your data. You can exercise these options by lodging an inquiry at support@linkpilot.work.
