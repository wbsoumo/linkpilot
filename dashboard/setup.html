WhatsApp Integration

Integrate WhatsApp via Meta Cloud API to automatically handle messaging channels.

Have you connected your phone number to Meta Developers?

Meta requires hosting phone numbers inside a WhatsApp Cloud API App in order to send automated messages programmatically.

Steps to Connect with Meta

Please follow this guide to connect your number to Meta before pasting details here:

1 Register as a developer on the Meta Developer Dashboard.
2 Click **Create App** (Select 'Other' & choose 'Business' app type).
3 Scroll down, find **WhatsApp**, click **Set Up** to link it.
4 Navigate to WhatsApp -> **API Setup** in the left menu.
5 Under Step 5, click **Add Phone Number**, fill your Business details, verify the number via SMS.
How to get these Meta Credentials:
• **Access Token**: Generate under Users -> System Users in Meta Business Settings. Ensure you grant `whatsapp_business_messaging` permissions.
• **IDs & Phone**: Navigate to WhatsApp -> API Setup in your Meta App Dashboard to copy Phone Number ID, WABA ID, and Display Number.
