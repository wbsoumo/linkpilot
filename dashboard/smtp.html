Gmail SMTP Setup Instructions

To send emails using your Gmail account, Google requires you to configure an **App Password** for security. Standard passwords will be blocked.

1 Go to your Google Account Dashboard.
2 Navigate to the **Security** tab in the left-hand menu.
3 Enable **2-Step Verification** under *How you sign in to Google* if it's not active.
4 Click **2-Step Verification**, scroll to the bottom, and select **App passwords**.
5 Enter `LinkPilot AI` in the app name, click **Create**, and copy the **16-character code**.
6 Use this code as your **Google App Password** in the credentials form below.
