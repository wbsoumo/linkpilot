Gmail SMTP Setup Instructions

To send emails using your Gmail account, Google requires you to configure an **App Password** for security. Standard passwords will be blocked.

1 Open your Google Account Dashboard.
2 Navigate to the **Security** tab in the left-hand menu.
3 Enable **2-Step Verification** under *Signing in to Google*.
4 Click **2-Step Verification**, scroll down and click **App passwords**.
5 Type `LinkPilot AI`, click **Create**, and copy the **16-digit app password**.
6 Paste this code as your **Google App Password** below.
