Data Deletion Instructions

Last Updated: July 6, 2026

LinkPilot AI is dedicated to protecting user data. If you have connected your Meta (Facebook/WhatsApp) Business Account with our services and wish to disconnect the integration or request a permanent deletion of your data logs, please follow the simple procedures detailed below.

1. Instant Deletion via CRM Dashboard

You can instantly disconnect your Meta Business permissions and delete your WhatsApp account access tokens, verifying secrets, and local broadcast queue settings directly from your LinkPilot Portal:

  1. Log in to your LinkPilot Dashboard.
  2. In the left sidebar, navigate to WhatsApp Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the form and click on the Disconnect Account button.
  4. Confirm the action. All active Meta system access tokens and verify configurations will be permanently purged from our database immediately.

2. Disconnect Integration via Facebook

To revoke LinkPilot’s API access permissions completely from your Meta Facebook Account, follow these instructions:

  1. Go to your Facebook Profile’s Settings & Privacy, and click on Settings.
  2. In the left-hand menu, click on Apps and Websites.
  3. Locate and search for the LinkPilot CRM or LinkPilot AI application.
  4. Click the Remove button next to the application name.
  5. Confirm by clicking Remove. This completely revokes the system auth scope.

3. Request Full GDPR/CCPA Account Deletion

Under GDPR and CCPA policies, you are entitled to request the permanent deletion of your complete user profile and database records. If you wish to delete all details (full name, encrypted credentials, synced contacts, timeline logs, and AI conversations history), follow these steps:

  • Send an email from your registered LinkPilot account email to our support team at: support@linkpilot.work.
  • State "Data Deletion Request" as your email subject.
  • Our Data Protection Officer will process your request within 24 to 72 hours. You will receive an automated confirmation email once all records are permanently deleted from our live databases and backup archives.
