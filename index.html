Frequently Asked Questions

How does LinkPilot AI work?

It's a Chrome extension that contextualizes LinkedIn posts. Clicking the AI Action button triggers our assistant to read the post, check your profile settings, and draft tailored emails, comments, or WhatsApp messages instantly.

Is it safe and compliant with LinkedIn?

Yes. Unlike scraping bots that automate browser behavior, LinkPilot AI acts as a writing assistant. It only drafts text that you physically review, edit, and send, keeping your account fully compliant.

Does it support Gmail SMTP?

Yes, it fully supports Gmail SMTP using Google App Passwords. We provide a step-by-step credentials wizard in the dashboard to secure App Password configuration in under two minutes.

Are my credentials secure?

Your SMTP credentials and API keys are encrypted in transit and at rest using AES-256-CBC cryptography. We do not store plain-text passwords on our servers.
